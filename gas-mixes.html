GAS MIX GUIDE // 20 – 50 M

Best breathing gas for UK recreational and sport dives at every 5 m step. Nitrox and Air only down to 40 m; Helitrox recommended below that for narcosis management. No hypoxic trimix is shown.

How to read this table: "Primary gas" is the best available blend for that depth. Where Nitrox gives a meaningful NDL advantage it is listed first; Air is shown where Nitrox options run out of headroom below their MOD. PPO₂ is calculated at the bottom depth using a 1.4 bar working limit. NDLs are approximate PADI/DSAT table values — always use your computer. Helitrox is shown at 45 m and 50 m where narcosis on Air becomes significant; it requires appropriate gas-blending training and certification.
Nitrox / EAN Enriched air — extended NDL, lower DCS risk
Air 21 % O₂ — universal, no MOD restriction to 57 m
Helitrox O₂ + He + N₂ — reduces narcosis, requires additional cert
Depth guide — 20 m to 50 m

20 m3.0 bar abs EAN36 (Nitrox 36) 36 % — 1.08 bar 28 m ~95 min Maximum NDL benefit at this depth. EAN36 is widely available at UK dive centres and ideal for shallow wreck and shore dives. Do not descend past 28 m on this mix without switching gas.
25 m3.5 bar abs EAN32 (Nitrox 32) 32 % — 1.12 bar 33 m ~65 min The most common Nitrox fill in the UK. Nearly doubles the air NDL at this depth. Used by the majority of sport divers on UK wrecks in the 18–30 m range. Stay clear of the 33 m MOD.
30 m4.0 bar abs EAN28 (Nitrox 28) 28 % — 1.12 bar 40 m ~50 min Comfortable headroom below MOD; NDL still roughly 2.5× better than Air. EAN32 is within its MOD at 30 m (MOD 33 m) but leaves almost no margin — EAN28 is the safer choice for a planned 30 m dive.
35 m4.5 bar abs EAN26 (Nitrox 26) 26 % — 1.17 bar 44 m ~28 min Nitrox still provides a useful NDL gain over Air (~14 min on Air at 35 m). MOD of 44 m gives a comfortable buffer. If a 26 % blend is unavailable, Air is the fall-back — narcosis at 35 m is manageable.
40 m5.0 bar abs EAN25 (Nitrox 25) or Air if EAN25 unavailable 25 % — 1.25 bar 46 m ~12 min EAN25 sits just inside its MOD and gives a modest NDL improvement over Air (~9 min). At 40 m narcosis becomes noticeable on Air; EAN25 still contains enough nitrogen to narce — plan conservatively. Air PPO₂ at 40 m = 1.05 bar.
45 m5.5 bar abs Air + Helitrox 25/25 recommended 21 % / 25 % — / 25 % 1.16 / 1.38 bar 57 m / 46 m ~7 min Air is legal and within PPO₂ limits, but narcosis at 45 m equates roughly to 4 glasses of wine — task loading increases significantly. Helitrox 25/25 (25 % O₂, 25 % He, 50 % N₂) reduces the equivalent narcotic depth to ~34 m. Requires Helitrox or equivalent blending cert. Note PPO₂ on 25/25 at 45 m = 1.38 bar — close to limit.
50 m6.0 bar abs Helitrox 21/35 or Air if no He available 21 % 35 % 1.26 bar 57 m ~5 min At 50 m on Air the equivalent narcotic depth is 50 m itself — impairment is serious and reaction times slow sharply. Helitrox 21/35 (21 % O₂, 35 % He, 44 % N₂) cuts the equivalent narcotic depth to ~33 m without changing the PPO₂ profile. This sits at the BSAC Sport Diver maximum depth; plan deco obligations carefully.
Oxygen limits: Working PPO₂ limit = 1.4 bar · Contingency / deco limit = 1.6 bar · MOD formula: (1.4 ÷ FO₂) × 10 − 10  |  Narcosis: Helium is non-narcotic; nitrogen and oxygen are narcotic at depth. Equivalent narcotic depth (END) = depth × (1 − He fraction).  |  NDLs shown are indicative PADI RDP values on a first dive; repeat dives, cold water and exercise all reduce the limit — use a calibrated dive computer.

How to calculate your SAC rate

1 Bar used × 24 (twin 12 L cylinders) = Litres used
2 Litres used ÷ Dive time (mins) = RMV
3 RMV ÷ Average Depth (bar abs) = SAC
RMV = Respiratory Minute Volume (litres per minute at depth) · SAC = Surface Air Consumption (litres per minute at the surface). A typical sport diver SAC is 15–20 L/min; well-trained divers often reach 10–12 L/min. Use your SAC rate to plan gas reserves and turn-pressure for any dive.
